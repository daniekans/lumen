Sixteen problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in
any order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- a <Term id="matrix-vector-product">matrix times a vector</Term>, by columns or by rows, and its
  shape;
- the <Term id="matrix">matrix</Term> of a map given by a formula, and images from the images of
  a basis;
- a map that is not <Term id="linear-transformation">linear</Term>, and the value that shows it;
- a layer $W\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}$, an <Term id="affine-map">affine map</Term>;
- a <Term id="matrix-product">matrix product</Term> of any shape, powers of a rotation, one
  described map after another, and the standard maps of the plane;
- a product of <Term id="transpose">transposes</Term>;
- a rotation of space about an axis, and maps between spaces of different sizes;
- a vector in the <Term id="kernel">kernel</Term>, the <Term id="rank">rank</Term>, and whether
  a vector is in the <Term id="image">image</Term>;
- one proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{gathered}
A\mathbf{x} = x_1\mathbf{a}_1 + \cdots + x_n\mathbf{a}_n, \qquad
AB = \begin{bmatrix} A\mathbf{b}_1 & \cdots & A\mathbf{b}_p \end{bmatrix}, \qquad
(AB)^T = B^TA^T, \\
R_\theta = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix}, \qquad
\ker A = \{\mathbf{x} : A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}\}, \qquad
\operatorname{im} A = \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{a}_1, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n\}.
\end{gathered}
$$
