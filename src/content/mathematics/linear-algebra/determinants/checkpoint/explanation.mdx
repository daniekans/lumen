Fifteen problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in
any order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- the <Term id="determinant">determinant</Term> of a $3 \times 3$ matrix by
  <Term id="cofactor">cofactors</Term> and of a $4 \times 4$ by elimination;
- the effect of row operations, and the value that makes a map collapse;
- points on one line, the area of a plot from its corners, and the area of an image;
- the <Term id="cross-product">cross product</Term>, the area of a triangle in space and the
  volume of a tetrahedron;
- four points in one plane through the <Term id="triple-product">triple product</Term>, and the
  plane through three points;
- torque on a bolt;
- the product rule with powers, scalars, transposes and inverses;
- one proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{gathered}
\det \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} = ad - bc, \qquad
\det(AB) = \det A \det B, \\
\det(cA) = c^n\det A, \qquad \det A^T = \det A, \\
\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w} =
\begin{bmatrix} v_2w_3 - v_3w_2 \\ v_3w_1 - v_1w_3 \\ v_1w_2 - v_2w_1 \end{bmatrix}, \\
\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) = \det[\mathbf{u}\ \mathbf{v}\ \mathbf{w}].
\end{gathered}
$$
