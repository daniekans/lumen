Sixteen problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in
any order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- a <Term id="linear-combination">linear combination</Term> and the weights that reach a target;
- a <Term id="vector-distance">distance</Term>, and a distance or cosine between feature vectors
  in four or five dimensions;
- a <Term id="dot-product">dot product</Term>, an angle and a projection;
- a dependence relation, or the value that makes two vectors
  linearly <Term id="linear-dependence">dependent</Term>;
- a plane through a point with a given <Term id="normal-vector">normal</Term>, a plane converted
  between parametric and normal form, and where a <Term id="parametric-line">line</Term> meets a
  plane;
- the value that stops three vectors from being a <Term id="basis">basis</Term>, and
  <Term id="coordinates">coordinates</Term> in a basis of the plane and of space;
- one proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{gathered}
\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert = \sqrt{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v}}, \qquad
\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w} = \lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert \, \lVert \mathbf{w} \rVert \cos\theta, \qquad
\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf{v}} \mathbf{w} = \frac{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{w}}{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v}}\,\mathbf{v}, \\
n_1 x + n_2 y + n_3 z = \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{p}, \qquad
[\mathbf{x}]_{\mathcal{B}} = (c_1, \ldots, c_n) \iff c_1\mathbf{b}_1 + \cdots + c_n\mathbf{b}_n = \mathbf{x}.
\end{gathered}
$$
