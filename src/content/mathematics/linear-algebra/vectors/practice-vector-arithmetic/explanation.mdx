Arithmetic with <Term id="vector">vectors</Term> in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and $\mathbb{R}^3$: work one
component at a time, and use the Pythagorean theorem for lengths.

## Answers

- Type a vector answer as its components in order, separated by commas, with or without
  parentheses: $(3, -1)$ or $(3, -1, 2)$. Fractions work too: $(\frac{3}{5}, -\frac{4}{5})$.
- Lengths and distances are exact: write $\sqrt{13}$ or $2\sqrt{3}$, not a decimal.
- When a question has two answers, give both, separated by a comma.

## Formulas

$$
a\mathbf{v} + b\mathbf{w} = (av_1 + bw_1, \ldots, av_n + bw_n),
$$

$$
\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert = \sqrt{v_1^2 + \cdots + v_n^2}.
$$

The <Term id="unit-vector">unit vector</Term> in the direction of $\mathbf{v}$ is
$\frac{\mathbf{v}}{\lVert \mathbf{v} \rVert}$, and the <Term id="vector-distance">distance</Term>
between two points is $\lVert \mathbf{w} - \mathbf{v} \rVert$. Finding the weights of
a <Term id="linear-combination">linear combination</Term> means solving one equation per component.
