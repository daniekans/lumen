The problems cover the whole unit. Exact answers such as $\frac{2\pi}{3}$ or $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ can be entered as they are written by hand; the checker compares values, so any equal form counts.

## Angles and the circle

$180^\circ = \pi$, and an angle of $\theta$ <Term id="radian">radians</Term> cuts an arc $s = r\theta$ on a circle of radius $r$. For an exact value, find the reference angle (the angle to the $x$-axis) and take the sign from the quadrant: cosine is negative on the left half, sine on the lower half, and tangent where they differ.

## Identities

The <Term id="pythagorean-identity">Pythagorean identity</Term> gives one ratio from another up to sign, and the quadrant gives the sign. The <Term id="angle-addition">addition</Term> and <Term id="double-angle">double-angle</Term> formulas:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(\alpha \pm \beta) &= \sin\alpha\cos\beta \pm \cos\alpha\sin\beta \\
\cos(\alpha \pm \beta) &= \cos\alpha\cos\beta \mp \sin\alpha\sin\beta \\
\sin 2\theta &= 2\sin\theta\cos\theta, \qquad \cos 2\theta = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

## Graphs and inverses

$y = A\sin(B(x - C)) + D$ has <Term id="amplitude">amplitude</Term> $\lvert A \rvert$, <Term id="period">period</Term> $\frac{2\pi}{\lvert B \rvert}$, and runs between $D - \lvert A \rvert$ and $D + \lvert A \rvert$. The <Term id="inverse-trig-functions">inverse functions</Term> return angles in $\left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ($\arcsin$, $\arctan$) and $[0, \pi]$ ($\arccos$). In one turn, $\sin x = c$ has the solutions $\arcsin c$ and $\pi - \arcsin c$, and $\cos x = c$ has $\pm\arccos c$, each moved into $[0, 2\pi)$.
