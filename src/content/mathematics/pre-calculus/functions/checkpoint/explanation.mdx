Ten problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- whether a relation is a <Term id="function">function</Term>;
- evaluating piecewise rules;
- domains of fractions and ranges of parabolas;
- formulas of <Term id="composition">compositions</Term> and <Term id="inverse-function">inverses</Term>;
- shifted graphs and where a point moves;
- one short proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)), \qquad f^{-1}(f(x)) = x,
$$

The <Term id="graph-transformation">transformation</Term> $a\,f\big(b(x - h)\big) + k$ moves a point $(x_0, y_0)$ of $f$ to

$$
\left(\frac{x_0}{b} + h,\ a y_0 + k\right).
$$

If a problem feels unfamiliar, the matching practice set in this unit has more of the same kind.
