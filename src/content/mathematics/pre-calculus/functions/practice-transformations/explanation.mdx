Every problem here uses one form:

$$
g(x) = a\,f\big(b(x - h)\big) + k.
$$

## Reading the form

- $h$ moves the graph right ($h > 0$) or left. Note the sign: $f(x + 3)$ means $h = -3$, left by $3$.
- $k$ moves it up ($k > 0$) or down.
- $a$ multiplies heights. $a < 0$ also reflects across the $x$-axis.
- $b$ divides horizontal distances by $b$. $b < 0$ also reflects across the $y$-axis.

## Following a point

A point $(x_0, y_0)$ on the graph of $f$ moves to

$$
\left(\frac{x_0}{b} + h,\ a y_0 + k\right).
$$

Find the new $x$ by asking which input feeds $f$ the old one, then apply the outside operations to
the output in order: multiply by $a$, then add $k$.

## Answering

Type formulas in $x$. Equivalent forms are accepted, so $(x - 2)^2 + 1$ and $x^2 - 4x + 5$ both
count.
