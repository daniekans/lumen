Two skills that work as a pair: <Term id="composition">composing functions</Term>, and undoing a function.

## Composition

$(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$ applies $g$ first. To evaluate, work from the inside out. To find the
formula, put the whole expression $g(x)$, in parentheses, wherever $f$ has $x$. For $f(x) = x^2 + 1$
and $g(x) = 2x - 3$:

$$
f(g(x)) = (2x - 3)^2 + 1 = 4x^2 - 12x + 10.
$$

## Inverses

To find <Term id="inverse-function">$f^{-1}$</Term>:

1. Write $y = f(x)$.
2. Solve for $x$ in terms of $y$.
3. Rename $y$ to $x$.

For a fraction, multiply out the denominator first, collect every term with $x$ on one side, then
factor $x$ out. Check any answer by composing: $f(f^{-1}(x))$ should simplify to $x$.

## Answering

Equivalent forms are accepted, so $\frac{x - 3}{2}$ and $\frac{1}{2}x - \frac{3}{2}$ both count. Type
a cube root from the keyboard's root key with index $3$.
