Two skills: evaluating a function, and finding which inputs and outputs it has.

## Evaluating

Replace every $x$ with the input, in parentheses. Parentheses matter for negative inputs and for
expressions. For $f(x) = x^2 - 3x$:

$$
f(-2) = (-2)^2 - 3(-2) = 10,
$$

$$
f(t + 1) = (t + 1)^2 - 3(t + 1).
$$

A piecewise rule is evaluated in two steps: find the condition the input satisfies, then use only
that piece.

## Domain

Start from all real numbers and remove what breaks the formula:

- under a square root, require $u \geq 0$;
- in a denominator, require $u \neq 0$.

When both apply, keep the inputs that satisfy every condition.

## Range of a parabola

In the form $f(x) = a(x - h)^2 + k$ the square is at least $0$, so $f(x) \geq k$ when $a > 0$ and
$f(x) \leq k$ when $a < 0$. The value $k$ itself occurs at $x = h$.

## Answering

Number answers accept fractions and decimals. For "list every number", separate the values with
commas, in any order.
