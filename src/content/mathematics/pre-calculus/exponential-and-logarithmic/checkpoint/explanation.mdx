Ten problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- evaluating <Term id="logarithm">logarithms</Term>, and expanding and combining them with the
  <Term id="logarithm-laws">logarithm laws</Term>;
- an <Term id="exponential-function">exponential function</Term> through two points, a
  <Term id="half-life">half-life</Term> and a <Term id="doubling-time">doubling time</Term>;
- exponential equations, with and without the <Term id="natural-logarithm">natural logarithm</Term>, and a logarithmic equation with an
  <Term id="extraneous-solution">extraneous solution</Term>;
- one short proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{gathered}
\log_b x = y \iff b^y = x, \qquad \log_b x = \frac{\ln x}{\ln b}, \\
\log_b (uv) = \log_b u + \log_b v, \qquad \log_b (u^k) = k \log_b u.
\end{gathered}
$$

Continuous growth at rate $r$ is $Pe^{rt}$. Check the roots of a logarithmic equation in the
original equation.

If a problem feels unfamiliar, the practice set in this unit has more of the same kind.
