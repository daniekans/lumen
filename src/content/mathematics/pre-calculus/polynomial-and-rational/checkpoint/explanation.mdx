## Useful facts

$$
\begin{gathered}
m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}, \\
ax^2 + bx + c = a\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 + c - \frac{b^2}{4a}.
\end{gathered}
$$

Odd multiplicity crosses, even multiplicity touches. For $\frac{p}{q}$, factor and cancel first:
cancelled factors are holes, the rest of the denominator gives vertical asymptotes. Compare degrees
for the behavior far away.
