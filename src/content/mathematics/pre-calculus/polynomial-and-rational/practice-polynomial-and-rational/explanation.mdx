Five skills: lines, quadratics, <Term id="end-behavior">end behavior</Term>, zeros, and <Term id="asymptote">asymptotes</Term>.

## Lines and parabolas

- The <Term id="slope">slope</Term> through two points is $\frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$; then
  $b = y_1 - m x_1$ gives $y = mx + b$.
- <Term id="completing-the-square">Complete the square</Term>: factor out $a$, add and subtract
$\left(\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2$ inside. The vertex is at $x = -\frac{b}{2a}$.
- A parabola with $a > 0$ has a minimum at its vertex; with $a < 0$, a maximum.

## Polynomials

- The leading term decides the ends: even degree, both ends the same way; odd degree, opposite
  ways. The sign of the leading coefficient picks the right end.
- In factored form, each factor $(x - r)^m$ gives a zero $r$ of <Term id="multiplicity">multiplicity</Term> $m$: odd crosses, even touches.

## Rational functions

- Factor, cancel common factors (each leaves a <Term id="hole">hole</Term>), and read vertical
  asymptotes from what stays in the denominator.
- A hole's height is the value of the simplified expression there.
- Degrees: numerator lower gives $y = 0$; equal gives the ratio of leading coefficients; one
  higher gives a slant asymptote, the quotient of long division.

## Answering

Lines are entered as $mx + b$ (with or without $y =$); any equivalent form is accepted. Lists take
numbers separated by commas, and fractions such as $\frac{3}{2}$ are accepted.
