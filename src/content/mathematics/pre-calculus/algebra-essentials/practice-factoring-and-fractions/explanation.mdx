Two skills: factoring polynomials, and working with fractions of polynomials.

## Factoring

- Take out the greatest common factor first: $6x^2 - 9x = 3x(2x - 3)$.
- For $x^2 + bx + c$, find $p$ and $q$ with $p + q = b$ and $pq = c$; then it equals $(x + p)(x + q)$.
- With a leading coefficient $a$, try $(ax + m)(x + n)$ and multiply back to check the middle term.
- $u^2 - v^2 = (u + v)(u - v)$.

## Rational expressions

- Simplify by factoring and cancelling common factors, never terms.
- Excluded values are the zeros of the original denominator, including those whose factor cancels.
  A cancelled factor leaves a hole; its $y$-coordinate is the simplified expression's value there.
- To add, rewrite over a common denominator and add the numerators.

## Answering

Factoring and simplifying are asked as choices, because an expanded form is equal to the factored
one. Some choices are equal but not fully factored; pick the complete factorization.
