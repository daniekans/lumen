Ten problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- zero and negative exponents, and the <Term id="simplest-radical-form">simplest form</Term> of a radical;
- factoring with a leading coefficient, and adding <Term id="rational-expression">rational expressions</Term>;
- rational and radical equations, where one candidate is <Term id="extraneous-solution">extraneous</Term>;
- quadratic and rational inequalities, and an <Term id="absolute-value">absolute value</Term> inequality;
- one short proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}, \qquad \sqrt{k^2 r} = k\sqrt{r}, \qquad
\lvert x - a \rvert < r \iff a - r < x < a + r.
$$

After squaring or clearing a denominator, check every candidate in the original equation. A
product or quotient changes sign only at a zero of one of its factors.

If a problem feels unfamiliar, the matching practice set in this unit has more of the same kind.
