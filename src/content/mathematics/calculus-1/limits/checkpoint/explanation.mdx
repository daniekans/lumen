Twelve problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- limits by <Term id="direct-substitution">direct substitution</Term>, factoring and
  rationalizing, and whether a two-sided <Term id="limit">limit</Term> exists;
- an <Term id="infinite-limit">infinite limit</Term> and a
  <Term id="limit-at-infinity">limit at infinity</Term>;
- <Term id="continuity">continuity</Term> of a piecewise function, a kind of
  <Term id="discontinuity">discontinuity</Term>, and a root interval from the
  <Term id="intermediate-value-theorem">intermediate value theorem</Term>;
- a limit built on $\frac{\sin u}{u} \to 1$;
- a comparison of growth rates;
- a $\delta$ from the <Term id="epsilon-delta">epsilon-delta definition</Term>, and one proof,
  graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{gathered}
\lim_{u \to 0} \frac{\sin u}{u} = 1, \qquad \ln x \ll x^p \ll b^x \quad (p > 0,\ b > 1), \\
\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L \iff \forall \varepsilon > 0\ \exists \delta > 0:\ 0 < |x - a| < \delta \implies |f(x) - L| < \varepsilon.
\end{gathered}
$$

If substitution gives $\frac{0}{0}$, remove the common factor first. If a problem feels unfamiliar,
the practice sets in this unit have more of the same kind.
