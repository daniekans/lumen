These problems use the rules so far, with no limits to compute.

## Rules

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(x^n\right) = nx^{n-1}, \qquad (af + bg)' = af' + bg'
$$

$$
(fg)' = f'g + fg', \qquad \left(\frac{f}{g}\right)' = \frac{f'g - fg'}{g^2}
$$

The <Term id="power-rule">power rule</Term> holds for whole $n$ at every $x$, and for negative
integers $n$ where $x \neq 0$. The <Term id="product-rule">product rule</Term> and
the <Term id="quotient-rule">quotient rule</Term> hold wherever $f$ and $g$ are differentiable, and the
quotient rule needs $g(x) \neq 0$.

## Method

1. Rewrite first: $\frac{3}{x^2} = 3x^{-2}$. A quotient with a constant numerator is often
   simpler as a negative power than with the quotient rule.
2. Pick the rule for the outermost operation: a sum, a product or a quotient.
3. Simplify at the end. Equivalent forms are accepted, so expanding is optional.

## Tangent lines

The <Term id="tangent-line">tangent line</Term> at $x_0$ passes through $(x_0, f(x_0))$ with slope
$f'(x_0)$:

$$
y = f(x_0) + f'(x_0)(x - x_0).
$$

Enter it as $mx + b$, or in any equivalent form.
