Thirteen problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- an error estimate with <Term id="differential">differentials</Term>;
- <Term id="critical-point">critical points</Term>, classified with the
  <Term id="first-derivative-test">first</Term> or
  <Term id="second-derivative-test">second derivative test</Term>, and absolute extrema on a closed
  interval;
- a point $c$ from the <Term id="mean-value-theorem">mean value theorem</Term>, and an
  <Term id="inflection-point">inflection point</Term>;
- two optimization models: a can with the least material and the closest point on a curve;
- limits with <Term id="lhopitals-rule">L'Hôpital's rule</Term>, a power form through logarithms,
  and a limit where the rule does not apply;
- a root with <Term id="newtons-method">Newton's method</Term>, and a start where it fails;
- one proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

- $dy = f'(x)\,dx$: the change along the tangent estimates the change in $f$.
- At a critical point: $f'$ changes sign from $+$ to $-$ at a maximum, from $-$ to $+$ at a
  minimum. On $[a, b]$, compare the critical points inside with both ends.
- Check the form before using the rule; for $1^\infty$, $0^0$ and $\infty^0$, find the limit of
  the logarithm.
- $x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$.
