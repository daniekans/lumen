Ten problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- counting relations, keys of two columns and functional dependencies;
- foreign keys that point at missing rows;
- the row counts of inner, right and full joins, and the rows of an anti join;
- joins on columns that repeat on both sides;
- a filter placed in `ON` or in `WHERE`;
- one short proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
|A \bowtie B| = \sum_k a_k \, b_k, \qquad |A \text{ LEFT JOIN } B| = \sum_k a_k \max(b_k, 1).
$$

NULL matches nothing. A key on one side means each row of the other side matches at most once.

If a problem feels unfamiliar, the matching practice set in this unit has more of the same kind.
