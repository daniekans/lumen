## Useful facts

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{partitions: } & P = d_1 \cdots d_k \leq \frac{B}{T} \\
\text{bucket: } & h \bmod n \\
\text{skew ratio: } & r = \frac{\max_j L_j}{N / n} \\
\text{bucketed join: } & \textstyle\sum_k a_k b_k \text{ pairs}
\end{aligned}
$$

From $n$ to $n + 1$ buckets, a key keeps its bucket only when $h \bmod n(n + 1) < n$. On a ring, a key
belongs to the first node at or after it, clockwise.
