Ten problems that cover the whole unit. Work them on paper first, then enter the answers in any
order and check the sheet at the end. The checkpoint passes at $80\%$.

## What it covers

- bytes and pages a scan reads in row and column layouts, and the offset of a value;
- dictionary and delta encoded sizes, and the entropy of a column;
- row groups skipped by footer statistics, and what a query reads with projection and pushdown;
- the row groups a filter reads in a sorted file;
- one short proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{pages: } & \left\lceil \frac{N w}{P} \right\rceil \\
\text{bits per code: } & b = \lceil \log_2 d \rceil \\
\text{entropy: } & H = \sum_k p_k \log_2 \frac{1}{p_k}
\end{aligned}
$$

A row group is skipped only when its $[\min, \max]$ rules out every row. Row $i$ (from $0$) is in
row group $\lfloor i / R \rfloor$.
