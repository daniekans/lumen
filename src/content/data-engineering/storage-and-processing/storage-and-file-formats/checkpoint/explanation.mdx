## What it covers

- bytes and pages a scan reads in row and column layouts, and the offset of a value;
- dictionary and <Term id="delta-encoding">delta</Term> encoded sizes, and the <Term id="entropy">entropy</Term> of a column;

- the <Term id="row-group">row groups</Term> a filter reads in a sorted file;
- one short proof, graded by you against the solution.

## Useful facts

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{pages: } & \left\lceil \frac{N w}{P} \right\rceil \\
\text{bits per code: } & b = \lceil \log_2 d \rceil \\
\text{entropy: } & H = \sum_k p_k \log_2 \frac{1}{p_k}
\end{aligned}
$$

A row group is skipped only when its $[\min, \max]$ rules out every row. Row $i$ (from $0$) is in
row group $\lfloor i / R \rfloor$.
