The formulas these problems use, for a column of $n$ values with $d$ distinct.

## Encodings

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{bits per code: } & b = \lceil \log_2 d \rceil \\
\text{dictionary + codes: } & d \cdot w + \frac{n b}{8} \text{ bytes} \\
\text{runs when sorted: } & d \\
\text{bits per delta: } & \lceil \log_2 (m + 1) \rceil, \ m = \max \Delta
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $w$ is the width of a value in bytes. A <Term id="run-length-encoding">run</Term> is a maximal stretch of equal neighbors.

## Information and entropy

A value with probability $p$ carries <Term id="information-content">$\log_2 \frac{1}{p}$ bits</Term>. The <Term id="entropy">entropy</Term> of the column is the
average,

$$
H = \sum_k p_k \log_2 \frac{1}{p_k},
$$

the floor for codes that write each value on its own. It is at most $\log_2 d$, reached when all
values are equally likely.

## Ratio

$$
\text{compression ratio} = \frac{\text{plain size}}{\text{stored size}}
$$
