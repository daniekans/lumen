The problems use the structure of a Parquet file: $N$ rows cut into <Term id="row-group">row groups</Term> of $R$ rows, one
column chunk per column in each row group, and a <Term id="parquet-footer">footer</Term> with the min and max of every chunk.

## Counting

$$
G = \left\lceil \frac{N}{R} \right\rceil \text{ row groups}, \qquad G \cdot C \text{ column chunks}
$$

Counting rows from $0$, row $i$ is in row group $\lfloor i / R \rfloor$.

## Skipping

A row group is <Term id="predicate-pushdown">skipped</Term> when its range $[\min, \max]$ cannot satisfy the filter:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x = v: & \quad v < \min \text{ or } v > \max \\
x > v: & \quad \max \leq v \\
x < v: & \quad \min \geq v \\
x \text{ between } a \text{ and } b: & \quad \max < a \text{ or } \min > b
\end{aligned}
$$

## What a query reads

The reader fetches the chunks of the columns it needs, the selected ones and the filter column, in
every row group it cannot skip. Without compression that is

$$
(\text{row groups read}) \cdot R \cdot \sum_{\text{needed}} w_j \text{ bytes.}
$$

## Formats

Parquet for large tables read a few columns at a time, Avro for records written one at a time with
a schema, JSON lines for nested records read whole, CSV for small exchanges with people and
spreadsheets.
